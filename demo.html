Option 1 — Flat list + filters

One screen, type chips, category tags

Best for

  • People who like everything in one place and use search/filter to slice it.

Strong

  • Simplest schema change — just add kind and category columns.
  • One mental model: it's all just rows.

Weak

  • Different workflows (idea capture vs daily todos) blend visually unless you actively filter.
  • Doesn't scale once you have 100+ items.

Option 2 — Ideas / Notes tabs

Two top-level lists, separate workflows

Best for

  • Treating "capture business ideas" and "manage daily work" as fundamentally different modes.

Strong

  • Clean separation of your two stated use cases.
  • Ideas tab keeps the original problem→solution structure intact.
  • Notes tab gets all the productivity features (status, daily review, streaks).

Weak

  • Categorization within each tab is flat (life areas as tags) — not as deep as projects.
  • If a project (e.g. Sakni) has BOTH ideas and notes, they live in different tabs.

Option 3 — Projects (your idea)

Top-level projects · mixed item types inside

Best for

  • How you actually think about your life — Sakni is a project, your business idea is a project, Daily/Life is a project.

Strong

  • Matches your mental model. Adding a new venture? New project.
  • Everything related to Sakni (notes, ideas, tasks, docs) lives together.
  • Scales — you can have 20 projects, each with its own context.

Weak

  • Slightly heavier UI (sidebar adds a column).
  • Need to commit to a project when capturing — but quick capture can default to your "Inbox" project.

Option 4 — Projects + sub-tabs ⭐

Projects sidebar · type tabs within each

Best for

  • The most powerful version — your projects idea + clean separation of notes / ideas / tasks within each project.

Strong

  • Inside Sakni you can flip between its notes (reference), ideas (problem→solution), and tasks (todos).
  • Daily review banner per project — focused.
  • Easiest to grow into AI features later (AI knows the project context).

Weak

  • The most complex schema (projects table + items with kind + status).
  • Two clicks to navigate (project → tab) instead of one — but you can pin a default.

My recommendation. Combines the structural win of your projects idea with the workflow clarity of typed tabs. Most "future-proof" of the four.
